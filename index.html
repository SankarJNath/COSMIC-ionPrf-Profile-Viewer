Load a folder of ionPrf files to begin

  1. Click Open folder… and choose a directory of COSMIC ionPrf_* NetCDF files (e.g. ionPrf_prov1_2021_308).
  2. Pick an occultation from the list on the left.
  3. Switch tabs to view each profile, then export figures (PNG/JPG at 300/600/1000 DPI) or the data to Excel.

Everything runs locally in your browser — no files are uploaded. Negative or −999 fill values are flagged, not silently plotted.
